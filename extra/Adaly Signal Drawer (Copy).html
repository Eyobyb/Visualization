Behaviour

Rules the build should keep.

Open
Intelligence sits ghosted under the composer at rest. 40px of downward scroll or swipe intent, ↓, or a click on the ghosts pops them up. Scroll up at the top, Esc, or the collapse button returns.
Three ways to ask
The hero query is for questions you already have; a "new in Intelligence" label under it hints at the stream. The hero fades as the stream rises. While browsing, a small New thread pill sits at the bottom center and takes you back up to the hero query. Ask about this on a card is the only way to ask about one signal. / does the same as New thread.
Event ops
The Focus layout on an event security workspace, fed by custom connectors (perimeter sensors, cameras, gates, ticketing, weather, patrol and incident logs). Same components and behaviour; it shows the stream is not limited to business analytics.
Focus layout
One column of briefs. Hovering a brief, or tabbing to it, expands it in place after a short pause; moving to another brief swaps which one is open, and leaving the stream closes it. Clicking goes straight to the full view. On touch, a tap expands instead.
Simplified view
Progressive disclosure. Every item starts as a brief: source, a short headline, one takeaway and a small visual hint, so the whole stream can be skimmed in seconds. Click a brief to expand it in place with the full explanation, its visualization and actions. Open details goes one level deeper to the full view.
Visual depth
Each item picks the form that fits its data: daily or hourly columns against a baseline, a table of the deals behind a number, a ranked list of accounts, a funnel, stacked stage bars, a source status list, or one card holding several KPIs.
Sources
Every item names the data-source agents behind it. Several names with "Collaborated" means they worked on it together. Any name or avatar opens that source's profile, with Interested and its recent intelligence.
Feedback
Thumbs up ranks similar items higher. Thumbs down asks one question, then dims the card. Both can be undone.
Quick chat
One window in the bottom right, scoped to the active signal. Asking about another signal reuses it and switches context. Each signal's quick chat is kept for the session, even after closing, and "recent" switches between them. Quick chats are never saved.
Intelligence
One ranked stream of signals (anomalies, watches, opportunities, trends, data quality), routines that have run, and modeling scenarios that have finished. The All / Signals / Routines / Scenarios filter narrows it without leaving the page.
One input
Starting a thread, from the hero, a card or a quick chat, glides the same query field down to the bottom of the thread, where it becomes the chat input. Going back glides it home.
Saved work
Open full thread is the save moment: it carries the signal, sources, evidence and every quick-chat message into a saved thread. Clicking a card's body opens its expanded intelligence view, which becomes a saved thread once you ask something. A new question from the hero or launcher also starts a saved thread.
Actions
Ask is the primary action. Email, sharing, watching, routing to Planning or Advanced Modeling and the ranking explanation live under More. Email only drafts; the user sends.
Ranking
Ordered by impact, urgency, novelty and confidence, boosted by sources you’re interested in and by feedback. New items slot into rank, not at the top by default.
Notifications
A new item from a source you’re interested in, or any critical one, also appears as a desktop notification built from the same object.
